Frequently Asked Questions

CinemaX is a streaming discovery platform that is completely free to use. We provide links and metadata from TMDB and Trakt. Some content may be available through third-party embedding services.
Video content is served through third-party embed servers (VidSrc, etc.). If one server doesn't work, we provide multiple server options. Try switching servers using the Server 1, Server 2, Server 3 buttons in the player.
You can browse all content without an account. However, to save titles to your watchlist, mark favourites, write reviews, and sync with Trakt, you'll need to sign in with your Google account.
Open the detail page of any movie or TV show and click the "My List" (+ icon) button. You must be signed in for this to work. You can view your saved titles on the My List page.
Trakt.tv is a free service that tracks what you watch. By connecting your Trakt account on the My List page, your watchlist and watch history will sync automatically across all your devices and apps.
Yes! CinemaX is fully optimised for mobile devices. The site is responsive and works on all screen sizes from 320px upwards. On mobile you'll see a bottom navigation bar for easy access to all sections.
In the video player, use the quality dropdown in the top bar to select from Auto, 1080p, 720p, 480p, or 360p. The actual quality depends on what the embed server provides.
All movie and TV show metadata (titles, posters, descriptions, ratings, cast) is sourced from The Movie Database (TMDB) API. Watchlist sync is provided by Trakt.tv. CinemaX does not host or stream any video content directly.
In the video player, click the CC (closed captions) icon in the top bar. Subtitle availability depends on the embed server. You can also set a default subtitle language in your Account Settings → Playback.
We take privacy seriously. Your account data is stored securely on Firebase. We do not sell your personal information. You can review our full Privacy Policy and request deletion of your data at any time.

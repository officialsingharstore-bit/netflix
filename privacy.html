Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Welcome to CinemaX ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including when you create an account, sign in with Google, add items to your watchlist, or submit reviews. This may include your name, email address, and profile photograph.

We also automatically collect certain information when you use our service, including:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent)
  • Device information (hardware model, operating system)
  • Usage data (titles viewed, searches performed, features used)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Personalise your content recommendations
  • Save your watchlist and favourites across sessions
  • Send you service-related communications (with your consent)
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage

Your account data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which employs industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. We do not store payment information as CinemaX is a free service.

Third-Party Services

We integrate with several third-party services whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — provides all content metadata
  • Trakt.tv — optional watchlist synchronisation
  • Google Firebase — authentication and data storage
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • VidSrc / embed providers — video streaming embeds

DMCA & Copyright

CinemaX does not host, store, or distribute any video content. All video is embedded from third-party sources. If you believe any content infringes your copyright, please contact us at dmca@cinemax.example.com with a DMCA takedown notice. We will respond within 48 hours.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please visit Account Settings or contact us directly.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember preferences, and analyse usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the service.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
